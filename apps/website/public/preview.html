What is live

Use the real workflow within testnet boundaries.

The Official Testnet runs the same user-control model intended for the product: a requester asks, the wallet shows authoritative facts, and the user approves and signs locally.

Wallet review

Understand connections, scopes, facts, context, risk presentation, rejection, approval, and local signing.

Integration contracts

Validate pairing, Payment Request creation, exact amount handling, expiry, idempotency, status, and events.

Operational clarity

Identify failures, unsupported capabilities, user support needs, and evidence required before a live decision.

What not to assume

Official Testnet is not mainnet.

Public availability does not turn test assets into money or expand capabilities beyond what the app and API explicitly label.

No blanket network or asset support

Only use combinations explicitly enabled and labeled in the exact wallet build being evaluated.

No production compliance conclusion

A non-custodial design is a product boundary, not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal review.

No recovery by the server

Loss of recovery material can mean loss of access. Cloud recovery is outside the current v1 boundary.

No merchant or agent signing authority

Pairing and connection tokens do not grant access to private keys or permission to sign for the user.
