How it works

A request is not permission.

VelarumPay separates the party asking for payment from the person who can approve and sign it.

01

Connect

Pair an app, merchant, or agent and review the limited scopes it requests.

02

Request

The connected service creates a standard Payment Request with an expiry and exact facts.

03

Review

Your wallet presents the amount, asset, network, recipient, fee, and transaction context.

04

Sign

You approve and sign locally. The requester can observe status, but cannot sign for you.

Transaction truth

The facts you sign outrank the story you were told.

Merchant and agent descriptions can add context, but they cannot replace chain facts. VelarumPay keeps those layers visibly separate.
