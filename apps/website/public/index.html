The wallet that lets AI agents pay — with you in control.

VelarumPay is a non-custodial wallet for AI-agent payments. Your agent requests a payment; you review it in plain language and sign it yourself. Your keys never leave your device.

How it works

1
Connect an agentScan a code to pair your AI agent with your wallet, and choose what it's allowed to do.
2
Agent requestsWhen a payment is needed, the agent sends a request to your wallet — it cannot pay on its own.
3
You review & signSee exactly who, how much, and why — in plain language — then approve with your own key.
4
DoneThe payment is broadcast and confirmed. You can pause or revoke any agent anytime.

Why it's safe

You hold the keys

Non-custodial by design. Private keys stay on your device — never on our servers, never with the agent.

Approval by default

Real payments require your signature. An agent can propose, but only you can approve.

Scoped & revocable

Each connection has limited permissions you choose, and you can pause or revoke it instantly.

Open protocol

Payment details follow a documented, validated format — what you sign is exactly what you see.
