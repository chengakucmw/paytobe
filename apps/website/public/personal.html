The experience

Four moments where you stay in control.

A connection can request a capability. It does not receive your key or permission to sign.

01

02

04

Your responsibilities

Non-custodial means your recovery material matters.

VelarumPay cannot recover a lost recovery phrase or reverse a correctly signed irreversible network transaction.

Protect the recovery phrase

Keep it offline, private, and separate from chat, email, cloud notes, screenshots, and support conversations.

Use device protection

Keep your operating system current and use the app's local authentication before sensitive actions.

Treat urgency as a warning

Reject requests that rely on pressure, surprise, mismatched recipients, or instructions to bypass review.

Common questions

Before you begin.

Can an AI agent spend by itself?

No. In the current product boundary, an agent can create and read requests within approved scopes, but real payments require the user to review and sign by default.

Does VelarumPay keep my private key?

No. Seed phrases, private keys, and derived signing material remain on the user's device. Platform services do not sign user transactions.

Can VelarumPay reverse a payment?

VelarumPay cannot promise reversal of an irreversible network transaction. Always verify the facts before signing.

How do I know which networks and assets work?

Use only the support labels shown for your exact Preview build. Examples and documentation are not a promise that every client can sign every network and asset combination.
